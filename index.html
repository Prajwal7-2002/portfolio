About & Philosophy

How I approach software engineering and systems research.

I focus on the systems internals of AI/ML inference: continuous batching schedulers, virtual memory managers, speculative decoding verifiers, and empirical hardware profiling.

Rather than treating LLM runtimes as black boxes or wrapping commercial APIs, I prefer building allocators, schedulers, and verification loops from scratch in pure PyTorch to understand exactly how tensors move across physical memory buses and compute registers.

I evaluate systems on real hardware with sample-size-aware percentiles (honest p99 for N ≥ 100), 95% confidence intervals, and synchronized GPU telemetry—documenting trade-offs, bottleneck limits, and negative results alongside speedups.
