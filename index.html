Featured · Document AI and RAG

Menmo

Upload your documents and chat with them. Every answer says where it came from (your documents, the web, general knowledge or memory) and cites its sources with page numbers.

  • An LLM router reads the recent chat and picks intent, tone and answer length, and rewrites follow-up questions so they stand on their own
  • Hybrid retrieval: dense embeddings + BM25 + optional cross-encoder re-ranking + 👍/👎 feedback from users
  • Answers from your documents when they're relevant; otherwise retries with a rewritten query, then web search with citations, then general knowledge
  • Memory: remembers facts you tell it and recalls them later; each user only ever searches their own documents
  • Web app with accounts, drag-and-drop uploads with progress, documents grouped by topic, and usage analytics

Clinic WhatsApp Assistant

Small language models · Privacy

EdgeSLM

Sorts company documents into Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted, served locally so documents never leave the machine.

  • LoRA fine-tune of SmolLM2-360M plus a regex policy layer for credentials and keys
  • Evaluated on a held-out, re-labelled set with 95% confidence intervals
  • What testing showed: a simple TF-IDF baseline beat the first fine-tune (91.7% vs 58.3%), and INT8 gave a 1.8× CPU speedup but cost too much accuracy
  • Now improving it: better training data and quantization that keeps accuracy, measured against the same held-out set

Predictive maintenance

VoltGuard

Warns that an EV motor is heading into thermal trouble from its electrical signals alone, before the temperatures you can't measure get there.

  • Built on 1.33 million readings from 69 recorded drives (Paderborn PMSM dataset)
  • XGBoost chosen over LSTMs for low-latency CPU inference; SHAP explanations drive an automatic RPM-limiting response
  • FastAPI service, live monitor, pytest suite and documented failure cases
