Newest · WhatsApp AI assistant

Clinic WhatsApp Assistant

A WhatsApp assistant for small clinics, demo built for a dental clinic in Dharwad. Patients ask questions and book in English, Kannada or Kanglish; the clinic sees bookings in a Google Sheet.

  • Live on WhatsApp's official Cloud API: real free-slot booking, cancel and reschedule, staff alerts
  • Reminders and follow-ups that respect WhatsApp's 24-hour rule and approved templates
  • Safety enforced in code: emergencies detected before the AI, no invented prices, no fake confirmations
  • 62-case evaluation set (100% on emergencies and fees) and 123 automated tests

Document AI · RAG

Menmo

Upload documents and chat with them. Every answer says where it came from (your documents, the web, general knowledge or memory) and cites sources with page numbers.

  • An LLM router picks intent, tone and length, and rewrites follow-up questions
  • Hybrid retrieval: dense + BM25 + optional cross-encoder + 👍/👎 feedback
  • Falls back to web search, then general knowledge; each user only searches their own files

Small language models · Privacy

EdgeSLM

Sorts company documents into Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted, served locally so documents never leave the machine.

  • LoRA fine-tune of SmolLM2-360M plus a regex policy layer for credentials and keys
  • Evaluated on a held-out, re-labelled set with 95% confidence intervals
  • Honest result: a TF-IDF baseline beat the fine-tune (91.7% vs 58.3%); INT8 gave a 1.8× CPU speedup but dropped accuracy to 25%

Predictive maintenance

VoltGuard

Warns that an EV motor is heading into thermal trouble from its electrical signals alone, before the temperatures you can't measure get there.

  • Built on 1.33 million readings from 69 recorded drives (Paderborn PMSM dataset)
  • XGBoost chosen over LSTMs for low-latency CPU inference; SHAP explanations drive an automatic RPM-limiting response
  • FastAPI service, live monitor, pytest suite and documented failure cases
